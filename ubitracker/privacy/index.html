Your money stays on your phone

In short: UbiTracker has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. The app connects to the internet only to download public market prices.

Who we are

UbiTracker is a personal money-tracking app developed by uberbora (publishing as Ubi). It follows Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Questions about this policy: uberyastudios@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your accounts, balances, credit cards and their statement settings.
  • Subscriptions, bills, income, expenses, transfers and installment plans.
  • Budgets, investments, price alerts, your language and your other settings.

This information is kept in the app's private storage. The developer cannot see it and never receives it.

Market prices from the internet

  • finans.truncgil.com: currency and gold prices in Turkish lira.
  • api.coingecko.com: prices of the cryptocurrencies you hold or set alerts for.
  • query1/query2.finance.yahoo.com: prices of the stocks and funds you hold or set alerts for.
  • www.isyatirim.com.tr: a backup source for Borsa İstanbul stock prices.

These requests contain only the name or symbol of an asset (for example "USD" or "THYAO.IS"). They never contain your amounts, balances or anything about you. Like any website, these services see an ordinary request with your IP address. If you set price alerts, the app makes the same kind of request in the background about every 15 minutes.

Backups

Save to phone writes a backup file to your phone's Documents/UbiTracker folder. Share opens Android's share menu, and the file goes only where you choose. The app never sends backups anywhere by itself.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is stored on the device only as a salted SHA-256 hash, and it is never put in backups. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by Android; the app never receives biometric data.

Home screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows your balance on the home screen. When the app lock is on, you can choose to hide the amounts on the widget in the app lock settings.

Notifications

Reminders for bills, card payments, low balance and price alerts are created on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

No selling, no sharing, no tracking

UbiTracker collects no personal data, shares nothing with third parties and sells nothing. It has no ads and no analytics.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling UbiTracker, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of your data. The developer has no copy, so there is nothing else to delete. Backup files you saved or shared stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

UbiTracker is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date.

İnternetten piyasa fiyatları

  • finans.truncgil.com: Türk lirası cinsinden döviz ve altın fiyatları.
  • api.coingecko.com: sahip olduğunuz veya alarm kurduğunuz kripto paraların fiyatları.

Bu isteklerde yalnızca varlığın adı veya sembolü bulunur (örneğin "USD" veya "THYAO.IS"). Tutarlarınız, bakiyeleriniz veya sizinle ilgili herhangi bir bilgi gönderilmez. Her web sitesinde olduğu gibi bu hizmetler IP adresinizi içeren sıradan bir istek görür. Fiyat alarmı kurarsanız uygulama aynı türden isteği arka planda yaklaşık 15 dakikada bir yapar.

Yedekler

Telefona kaydet yedek dosyasını telefonunuzdaki Documents/UbiTracker klasörüne yazar. Paylaş Android'in paylaşım menüsünü açar ve dosya yalnızca seçtiğiniz yere gider. Uygulama yedekleri kendiliğinden hiçbir yere göndermez.

Uygulama kilidi

Uygulama kilidini açarsanız PIN'iniz cihazda yalnızca tuzlanmış SHA-256 özeti olarak saklanır ve yedeklere konmaz. Parmak izi ve yüz tanıma tamamen Android tarafından yürütülür; uygulama biyometrik veri almaz.

Ana ekran widget'ı

Widget'ı eklerseniz bakiyeniz ana ekranda görünür. Uygulama kilidi açıkken, kilit ayarlarından widget'taki tutarları gizlemeyi seçebilirsiniz.

Bildirimler

Fatura, kart ödemesi, düşük bakiye ve fiyat alarmı hatırlatmaları telefonunuzda oluşturulur. Bunları uygulamadan veya Android ayarlarından kapatabilirsiniz.
