Your streams stay on your PC

In short: UbiClipper is a Windows app that runs on your own computer. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. Your videos, clips and transcripts never leave your PC unless you post a clip yourself. The app goes online only to download its AI models once, to fetch videos from links you paste, and to upload clips to the accounts you connect.

Who we are

UbiClipper is developed by uberbora (publishing as Ubi). It follows Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Questions about this policy: uberyastudios@gmail.com.

What happens on your PC

Finding moments, speech to text, speaker colours, the AI judge, subtitles and video editing all run on your computer. Nothing you open in UbiClipper is sent to us or to any AI service.

UbiClipper keeps these in the .ubiclipper folder in your Windows user folder:

  • Your settings, your webcam layout and your list of misheard-word fixes.
  • The AI models it downloaded.
  • Videos downloaded from links, until you clear them in Settings.
  • A log file that helps with bug reports. It stays on your PC unless you send it to us.
  • Connected accounts: the sign-in tokens and the app keys you entered, encrypted with Windows so that only your Windows account can read them.

Finished clips, subtitles and transcripts are saved in the export folder you choose.

Posting to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube

Connecting an account is optional. You sign in on the platform's own page in your browser, using a developer app you created yourself. UbiClipper asks only for what it needs to post:

  • TikTok: your basic profile and permission to upload videos.
  • Instagram: your basic profile, permission to publish content, and the Facebook Page and business account it is linked to.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and to read your channel.

A clip is uploaded only when you press post. What happens to it after that is covered by the platform's own privacy policy. Disconnect in the Accounts tab deletes the saved sign-in from your PC (the app keys you entered stay, so reconnecting is quick); you can also remove UbiClipper's access in each platform's settings.

No selling, no sharing, no tracking

UbiClipper collects no personal data, shares nothing with third parties and sells nothing. It has no ads and no analytics.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling UbiClipper asks whether to delete the .ubiclipper folder too. You can also delete that folder yourself at any time. The developer has no copy, so there is nothing else to delete. Clips you exported or posted stay where you put them.

This website

The download page asks GitHub for the newest version so the button always gets the right file, and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. It sets no cookies; it only remembers your language choice in your browser.

Children

UbiClipper is not directed at children under 13.

TikTok, Instagram ve YouTube'a paylaşma

Hesap bağlamak isteğe bağlıdır. Kendi oluşturduğunuz bir geliştirici uygulamasıyla, platformun kendi sayfasında tarayıcınızda giriş yaparsınız. UbiClipper yalnızca paylaşmak için gerekenleri ister:

  • TikTok: temel profiliniz ve video yükleme izni.
  • Instagram: temel profiliniz, içerik yayımlama izni ve bağlı olduğu Facebook Sayfası ile işletme hesabı.
  • YouTube: video yükleme ve kanalınızı okuma izni.

Bir klip yalnızca paylaş'a bastığınızda yüklenir. Sonrasında ona ne olduğu platformun kendi gizlilik politikasına tabidir. Hesaplar sekmesindeki Bağlantıyı kes, kayıtlı girişi bilgisayarınızdan siler (girdiğiniz uygulama anahtarları kalır, böylece yeniden bağlanmak kolaydır); UbiClipper'ın erişimini her platformun ayarlarından da kaldırabilirsiniz.
